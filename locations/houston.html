Canary Bakery in Houston

Canary Bakery bakes fresh bread, pastries, and cakes every morning for the local community. Stop by our Houston location for sourdough loaves.

We also serve Dallas and San Antonio.

What we bake

Canary Bakery bakes fresh sourdough every morning. The bakery also bakes jalapeno cheddar loaves daily. Kolaches fill the pastry case each day. Our Montrose shop supplies three cafes on Westheimer.

Cakes and large orders

Canary Bakery sits at 1919 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX 77098. Wedding cakes require booking six weeks in advance. Call (713) 555-0188 to place an order.

Fresh Bread, Pastries & Cakes Daily

Canary Bakery opens Tuesday to Sunday. The bakery opens at 7am each day. The bakery closes at 3pm each day. Street parking is available on Hopkins St. A long communal table seats twenty people. Free wifi is available for guests.
