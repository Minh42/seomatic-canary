Canary Bakery in Austin

Our Austin shop on South Congress bakes sourdough, croissants and cakes every morning for the neighborhood. The ovens start at 4am, so the first loaves are still warm when the doors open at 7.

What we bake in Austin

Every day we bake country sourdough, seeded rye and a soft sandwich loaf, plus butter croissants, pain au chocolat and cinnamon rolls. On weekends the counter adds breakfast tacos on fresh flour tortillas, kolaches with sausage and jalapeño, and a rotating fruit tart that follows what the farmers bring in: peaches in early summer, figs in August, pecans and pumpkin in the fall.

All bread is naturally leavened with a starter we have kept alive since the first market stall. Doughs ferment for at least eighteen hours, which gives the crumb its open texture and makes the loaves keep for three to four days in a paper bag.

Cakes and large orders

Birthday cakes, sheet cakes for the office and bread for events can be ordered at the counter or by phone. Give us 48 hours for cakes and 24 hours for more than ten loaves. We bake gluten-reduced and vegan options on request, though the kitchen handles wheat, nuts, dairy and eggs every day.

Visit us in Austin

The shop is open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm, and closed on Mondays so the team can rest and feed the starter. There are a few tables inside and a shaded bench out front. Street parking is free before 9am, and the bus stop is a two minute walk away.

Coffee comes from a small roaster across town, brewed as drip, espresso or cold brew. Bring your own cup and the coffee is a dollar off.

Questions

Do you sell day-old bread? Yes: whatever is left at closing goes in a half-price basket by the door, and anything after that is given to a local food bank. Can I order ahead? Yes, by phone or at the counter, for pickup any day we are open.
