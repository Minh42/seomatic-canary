Canary Bakery in Dallas

Canary Bakery bakes country sourdough, seeded rye, kouign-amann, and pecan sticky buns fresh every morning in Dallas, TX. Stop by the bakery or find us at the Knox-Henderson farmers market each Saturday.

What We Bake

Canary Bakery bakes country sourdough with a deep, tangy flavor and crackling crust every morning. Our seeded rye carries caraway and whole grains through each dense, fragrant slice. The kouign-amann emerges from the oven in layers of caramelized sugar and salted butter. Pecan sticky buns glaze over with dark molasses and toasted nuts. We bake the bread for the Knox-Henderson farmers market every Saturday.

Our Hours and Parking

Canary Bakery opens Tuesday through Sunday. Doors open at 7am each morning. The bakery closes at 2pm daily. The shop stays closed every Monday. Free parking sits behind the shop on Willis Ave.
