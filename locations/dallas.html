Canary Bakery in Dallas

Canary Bakery bakes fresh bread, pastries, and cakes every morning for the local community in Dallas. Start your morning with sourdough loaves, croissants, cinnamon rolls, or seasonal fruit tarts.

What we bake

Canary Bakery bakes country sourdough loaves every morning for the local community. We bake seeded rye bread fresh each day for our customers. Our kouign-amann comes out of the ovens each morning. We make pecan sticky buns daily for anyone who walks in. We bake the bread for the Knox-Henderson farmers market every Saturday.

Cakes and large orders
