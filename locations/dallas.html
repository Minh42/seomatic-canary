Canary Bakery Dallas: location, hours and pre-orders

Canary Bakery

2100 Main Street, Dallas 75201. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm. Closed Mondays.

Country sourdough, seeded rye, butter croissants, kolaches, pecan pie in the fall. Drip, espresso and cold brew from a Deep Ellum roaster.

Cakes need 48 hours notice. More than ten loaves need 24 hours. Order by phone or at the counter.

Free lot behind the shop. DART stop at Main and Ervay, two minutes away. Twelve seats inside and a patio.

Half-price day-old basket at closing. Leftovers go to a local food bank.
