Canary Bakery in Dallas

Canary Bakery bakes country sourdough, seeded rye, kouign-amann and pecan sticky buns fresh in Dallas, TX. We also bake bread for the Knox-Henderson farmers market every Saturday.

What We Bake

Canary Bakery bakes country sourdough every morning. The loaves have a crackling crust and open crumb. We use a long cold fermentation for depth.

Our seeded rye carries caraway and toasted seeds throughout. The dense, moist crumb pairs with cured fish. We bake it fresh each day.

Kouign-amann emerges from the oven caramelized and layered. Butter and sugar laminate the Breton pastry. The edges turn lacquered and crisp.

Pecan sticky buns glaze in brown sugar and butter. Whole pecans crown each spiral. We proof them overnight for tenderness.

We bake the bread for the Knox-Henderson farmers market every Saturday. The stand opens early in Dallas, TX. Locals line up for warm loaves.

Cakes and Large Orders

Hours, Parking & Location

Canary Bakery opens Tuesday through Sunday. Doors open at 7am each morning. Service ends at 2pm every day. The shop stays closed on Mondays. Free parking sits behind the shop on Willis Ave.
