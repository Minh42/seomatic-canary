Canary Bakery in Dallas

Canary Bakery bakes fresh bread, pastries, and cakes every morning in Dallas, TX. Stop by to pick up sourdough loaves, croissants, cinnamon rolls, and seasonal fruit tarts.

What we bake

Canary Bakery bakes country sourdough. We bake seeded rye. We bake kouign-amann. We bake pecan sticky buns. We bake the bread for the Knox-Henderson farmers market every Saturday.

Cakes and large orders

Canary Bakery sits at 2410 N Henderson Ave, Dallas, TX 75206. The bakery accepts sheet cake orders for offices with 48 hours notice.

Hours and Parking

Canary Bakery opens Tuesday through Sunday. Doors open at 7am each morning. The bakery closes at 2pm daily. The shop stays closed on Mondays. Free parking sits behind the shop on Willis Ave.
